Figure 4. Proportion of sequences for which the bucket-count chi-squared test yields p > 0.05. Rows correspond to sequence lengths of4 Mb and8 Mb; columns use B=101,199,499 buckets, with k=6–14. Each plotted percentage is the number of non-rejections among100 sequences divided by100, expressed as a percentage. Numeric data are unchanged from the accepted original bucket-count experiment; no GC or low-complexity diagnostic data are substituted. A non-rejection is not proof of uniformity. Curves connect the observed proportions; no standard deviation or new statistical test is introduced.
