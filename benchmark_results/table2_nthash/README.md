# Table 2 and Supplementary Table S3

[The complete 40-condition table](table2_s3_all40.tsv) compares KSSD-Array and ntHash on continuous DNA. It reports each method's arithmetic mean throughput and sample standard deviation over five repeats. The 95% confidence interval applies to the paired throughput ratio.

The [primary results](../supplementary_s2_nthash/primary/) contain the 400 source observations and their summaries. The [ntHash workflow](../../reproducibility/table2_nthash/README.md) documents the benchmark source and the small-input check. [Supplementary Table S4](../supplementary_s2_nthash/lto/) reports a separate comparison with link-time optimization enabled and disabled.

The historical filename `Table2_S4_complete.tsv` predates the final numbering. Its contents are byte-identical to `table2_s3_all40.tsv`.
